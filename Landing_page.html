Welcome to the Midnight Library

Quite dark isn't it? That's the point of this website! So that you can read in the dark without a blinding flash of light.

This site can be used as a notepad or an archive and whatnot. Store your stories or whatever wall of text in here if you want and be able to access anytime. Our specialty is "Do it in the dark". Only one rule: There is no light theme. So have fun and make sure to share this to others.

Write stories, notes, ideas, or whatever comes to mind. The Midnight Library is your personal space for words.

Keep your writings organized and accessible whenever you return to the library.
