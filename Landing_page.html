Welcome to the Midnight Library

Quite dark isn't it? That's the point of this website! So that you can read in the dark without a blinding flash of light.

This site can be used as a notepad or an archive and whatnot. Store your stories or whatever wall of text in here if you want and be able to access it anytime or share them publicly or privately. Our specialty is "Do it in the dark". Only one rule: There is no light theme.

📖 Write

Write stories, notes, ideas, writing styles, or whatever comes to mind. The Library is built for those needs.

🗄️ Archive

Keep your writings organized and accessible whenever you return to the Library. You may share it publicly or just keep things in private.

🌙 Stay in the Dark

No bright screens. No light theme. Just a dark environment designed for comfortable reading and writing.
